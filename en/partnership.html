Company Introduction · EN

WELLGOING Co., Ltd. is a premium healthcare brand that provides dignified care and sustainable care solutions based on “Compassionate Technology.” Its flagship product, Bichae Body Shield, is a premium combination cleansing and moisturizing body tissue that cleanses, deodorizes, moisturizes and soothes — even where water is hard to come by. Recognized across hospitals, home healthcare, nursing homes and travel.
