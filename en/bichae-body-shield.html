FAQ

Questions
before you buy

01How is it different from an ordinary wet wipe?

A wet wipe only wipes. Bichae Body Shield is an MFDS-notified cosmetic that cleanses, deodorises, moisturises and soothes. The 30 × 30 cm sheet holds 45 mL of essence, so one sheet washes a wide area and finishes with moisture, no rinsing. People whose skin dried out from alcohol-based bath wipes notice the difference most.

02Can one sheet do the whole body?

At 30 × 30 cm and 100 gsm, the embossed sheet is far larger and thicker than an ordinary wipe. Buyers describe it as “enough for the whole body” and “like a towel”.

03Does it feel sticky without rinsing?

It is designed to absorb quickly without stickiness. In the hospice survey, 78% named the ‘fresh finish’ as a strength.

04Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

In the human patch test at HUMAN Skin Clinical Trial Center it scored a skin irritation index of 0.00 — non-irritating (Excellent). It contains no preservatives, and hinoki water (EWG grade 1) and natural aroma oils help soothe the skin. No product suits every skin, so discontinue use if irritation occurs.

05What does it smell like?

A soft forest-rain scent from natural lavender and peppermint oils — chosen to soothe patients and carers weary of the smell of rubbing alcohol.

06Can it be used on the face or on wounds?

It is a body-care cosmetic. We advise against use on the face, mucous membranes, wounds or eczema. If irritation occurs, stop using it and consult a doctor.

07Shelf life and storage?

Each sheet is individually wrapped, so the essence stays fresh until opened. The expiry date is printed on the package and pouch; store in a cool place away from direct sunlight.

08Can institutions order on a regular basis?

Yes — we supply hospitals, care facilities and hospices on a regular basis and in bulk. Choose ‘Products · Bulk purchase’ on the contact page.
