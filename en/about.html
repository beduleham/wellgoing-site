A Message from Our CEO

“We began with the dignity
of those who cannot
wash with water.”

In hospice wards and nursing homes, I have long watched people who cannot wash with water — skin roughened by rubbing alcohol, and the small things that the hours without washing take from a person.

WELLGOING began with the wish that dignity be kept even in those hours. The AI that organises records and passes them to the next person, and the single sheet that washes without water, come from the same wish: to lighten the hands of those who care, and to keep the grace of those who are cared for. We call it Compassionate Technology.

We will keep our promises — small, honest, one at a time.

Son Young Ok

Chief Executive Officer

Son Young Ok
